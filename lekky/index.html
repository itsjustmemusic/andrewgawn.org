Lekky

Prepaid electricity, under control

An Android app for prepaid (pay-as-you-go) electricity meters. Add your readings and top-ups; Lekky shows how much credit you have now, how long it will last, and what your electricity really costs.

Coming soon to Google Play

What Lekky does

Everything comes from the readings and top-ups you enter, so the figures are yours, not guesses.

£Know where you stand

  • Your estimated balance now and the days until your meter reaches zero
  • Averages from your own readings: recent, 7-day, 30-day, weighted and total, with optional seasonal adjustment

!Never run out

  • Low balance and time-to-top-up alerts
  • Reading reminders on the schedule you choose
  • Quiet hours and snooze, so alerts don't wake you

↗See what you spend

  • What you spent yesterday, last week, last month or last year
  • Estimates for the days ahead, or any dates you pick
  • A cost-per-day graph

✓Catch mistakes

  • Validation spots readings that don't add up, duplicate entries and long gaps
  • Fix them in a tap

Your data stays yours

Lekky has no servers. What you enter stays on your phone.

Free, with an optional tip

Lekky is free. If it helps you, you can leave a tip in the app; a tip is a thank-you and unlocks nothing. Lekky Pro, a separate app with extra features, is on the way.
